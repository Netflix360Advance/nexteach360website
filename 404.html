About NexTeach 360™ & NextBoard™

NexTeach 360™ is an AI-powered K-12 education platform built natively for Google Classroom and Google Workspace. Streamline lesson creation, AI paper grading, student telemetry, and multilingual parent communication with client-side PII tokenization for full FERPA and COPPA compliance.

Built for teachers: NextBoard™ AI Lesson Architect generates curriculum-aligned modules in 60 seconds, NextGrade™ automates rubric-based grading with OCR, and NextShield™ scrubs PII locally on device CPU/GPU.
